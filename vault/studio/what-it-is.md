# What Northbeam Studio is

An automation studio for small professional-services firms: it finds the repetitive work in an
office and replaces it with automations that a person still supervises.

- Started in July 2025. The founder does the work; a freelance designer joins on demand for
  client-facing screens.
- Typical builds: invoice and delivery-note intake, email triage with drafts a person approves,
  order intake from WhatsApp, and internal assistants that answer from the firm's own documents.
- Stack: n8n (self-hosted), Python, and the language model each client chooses, always on the
  client's own account.

## What it does not do

- Marketing chatbots, ad campaigns or websites.
- Agents that act on their own with no human review (see [red lines](red-lines.md)).
