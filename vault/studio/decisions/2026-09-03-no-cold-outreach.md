# No cold outreach for the studio

Two test batches of 40 cold emails in August 2026 got one reply and no calls. All three clients
came from LinkedIn content and a partner referral. Cold email to companies also needs a
documented legitimate-interest assessment under the GDPR, which is work for no return.

Instead: LinkedIn posts twice a week, partner accountancies, and the diagnosis form on the
website. See [acquisition](../acquisition.md).
