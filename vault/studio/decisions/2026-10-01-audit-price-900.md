# The automation audit goes up to €900 + VAT

The three audits delivered took 14 to 16 hours each, not the 10 that €600 assumed, and they now
exist as case studies, which was the reason for the low price.

- From 2026-10-01 the audit costs €900 + VAT and includes a recorded walkthrough of the findings.
- The credit against a build is kept, now limited to builds hired within 60 days.
- Proposals sent before 2026-10-01 keep the €600 price until 2026-10-31.
