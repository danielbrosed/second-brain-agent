# Studio offer and pricing

Three products, always in this order: audit, build, care plan.

## Automation audit

- €900 + VAT, fixed price, two weeks.
- Delivers a map of the firm's processes, the three automations worth building first with the
  hours each would save (estimated from one week of baseline the client records), and a recorded
  walkthrough of the findings.
- If the client hires a build within 60 days, the audit fee is credited against it.

## Build

- From €3,500 + VAT, fixed price per agreed scope.
- 50% at kick-off, 50% at delivery. Thirty days of fixes included after go-live.

## Care plan

- €450 + VAT per month: monitoring, fixes and up to four hours of changes.
- Three-month minimum, then cancel with 30 days' notice.

## Not included

- Third-party API and model usage: billed by the provider directly to the client's own account.
- Hardware.
- Support outside 9:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday.
