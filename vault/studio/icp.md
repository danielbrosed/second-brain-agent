# Who buys from the studio

**Professional-services firms of 5 to 50 people in Spain**: accountancies, law firms, dental
clinics, architecture studios. The owner or the office manager decides and signs.

## Trigger

They are about to hire someone for admin work, or a busy season is coming (the quarterly tax
deadline for accountancies).

## Pain, in their words

"We copy the same data into three places." "Nobody knows which version of the invoice is the
good one."

## Budget

€3,000 to €10,000 a year for tools and help, usually without having called it "automation".

## Not a fit

- Companies above 250 people: procurement cycles longer than the project.
- E-commerce and dropshipping.
- Anyone who wants a fully autonomous agent with no human review.
