# Where studio clients come from

## Channels

1. **LinkedIn posts** by the founder, every Tuesday and Thursday. Each post ends with an
   invitation to the diagnosis call.
2. **The website form** at northbeam.example/diagnosis. It creates a person and a deal in Attio,
   in the stage "New".
3. **Partner accountancies** that refer their clients. The partner earns 10% of the client's
   first invoice, paid once the client has paid.

No cold email and no cold LinkedIn messages: see the
[decision of 2026-09-03](decisions/2026-09-03-no-cold-outreach.md).

Of the three closed clients, two came from LinkedIn and one from a partner referral.

## The diagnosis call

Free, 30 minutes, booked through Cal.com. It ends with a yes or no to the audit, never with a
discount.

## Pipeline stages in Attio

New → Diagnosis booked → Audit proposed → Audit won → Build proposed → Build won, or Lost at any
stage with a reason.
