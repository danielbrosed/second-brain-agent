# What Field Notes is

A weekly newsletter on practical automation for small businesses, written by the founder.

- Goes out every Tuesday at 8:00 CET. First issue on 2025-11-04.
- Each issue has the same shape: one real case, one tool, one template the reader can copy.
- The public archive is at fieldnotes.northbeam.example.
- Its job is to bring clients to the studio: every issue ends with a link to the diagnosis call.
