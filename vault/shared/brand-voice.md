# Brand voice

One voice for the three businesses: a person who builds things, talking to another person who
runs a business.

- Write like you talk. Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- Concrete over clever: a measured number beats an adjective.
- Banned words: revolutionary, game-changer, seamless, cutting-edge, unlock, supercharge.
- No emojis in proposals, invoices or client documents.
- B2B prices always say "+ VAT". Tidepool's public prices also say "+ VAT".
- Spanish-speaking clients get Spanish; Catalan if they write in Catalan; English otherwise.
- Sign-off for the studio and the newsletter: "— Northbeam".
