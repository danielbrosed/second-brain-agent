# Invoicing

Northbeam invoices as a sole trader registered in Spain.

## Who gets which tax

- Spanish businesses: 21% VAT on every invoice.
- Businesses elsewhere in the EU with a valid VAT number (checked in VIES before invoicing):
  no VAT, reverse charge, and the invoice says so.
- Customers outside the EU: no VAT.

## Tools and numbering

- Studio work and Field Notes sponsorships are invoiced from Invoice Ninja, in one series per
  year: `NB-2026-001`, `NB-2026-002`…
- Tidepool subscriptions are invoiced automatically by Stripe Billing, not by Invoice Ninja.
- A number is never reused or deleted. Mistakes are fixed with a corrective invoice in the
  series `R-2026-001`…

## Payment terms

- 15 days, by bank transfer, unless a proposal says otherwise.
- Studio builds: 50% at kick-off and 50% at delivery (see the
  [studio offer](../studio/offer-and-pricing.md)).
