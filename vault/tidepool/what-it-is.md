# What Tidepool is

A booking and membership app for independent yoga and pilates studios. The studio publishes its
schedule; members book, pay for single classes or class packs, and join a waitlist that promotes
them automatically when a spot opens.

- Beta from 2026-03-02; paid plans since 2026-04-01.
- The app lives at app.tidepool.example; the public site at tidepool.example.
- Available in Spanish, Catalan and English.
- Built with Next.js and Supabase, hosted on Vercel (see [platforms](platforms.md)).
