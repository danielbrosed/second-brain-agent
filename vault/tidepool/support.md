# Tidepool support

- Email to support@tidepool.example, answered within one business day (Monday to Friday,
  9:00–18:00 CET).
- In-app chat, only on the Studio plan.
- No phone support.
- Incidents are posted on status.tidepool.example.

## Refunds

- Monthly plans are not refunded for the month already started.
- Annual plans are refunded pro rata if the studio asks within 30 days of paying.
