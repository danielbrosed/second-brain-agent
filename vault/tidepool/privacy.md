# Tidepool and personal data

- Each studio is the controller of its members' data; Tidepool is the processor.
- Studios accept the data processing agreement (version 2026-03) when they sign up.
- Data is stored in the EU (Supabase, Frankfurt).
- Sub-processors: Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, Resend and Sentry.
- Member data is deleted 30 days after a studio closes its account.
- No health data: member profiles have fixed tags instead of free-text notes (see the
  [decision](decisions/2026-06-02-no-free-text-member-notes.md)).
