# Two plans, not three: Starter €29 and Studio €79

Solo took 9 of the first 30 studios, and those studios upgraded far less than expected: the cheap
plan was eating Starter instead of feeding it. Support work per studio was the same on every plan.

- Solo is retired for new studios. Studios already on it keep €19 until 2026-12-31.
- Starter drops from €39 to €29, low enough for teachers who would have chosen Solo.
- Studio drops from €89 to €79.
