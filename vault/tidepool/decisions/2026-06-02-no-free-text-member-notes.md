# Member profiles have tags, not free-text notes

During the beta, studios used the free-text notes on member profiles to write down injuries and
medical conditions. That is health data, a special category under article 9 of the GDPR, which
would need a different legal basis and a higher level of security than Tidepool offers.

Free-text notes are disabled. Studios get fixed tags instead: "beginner", "returning" and
"prefers morning classes". See [privacy](../privacy.md).
