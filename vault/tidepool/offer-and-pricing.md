# Tidepool plans and pricing

Two plans since 2026-09-14 (see the [decision](decisions/2026-09-14-two-plans.md)).

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | €29/month + VAT | one location, up to 150 active members, online payments, class packs |
| Studio | €79/month + VAT | up to three locations, unlimited members, waitlists with automatic promotion, staff accounts, payroll export for instructors |

- Annual billing: two months free (Starter €290/year, Studio €790/year, + VAT).
- 14-day free trial, no card required.
- Card fees are Stripe's and paid by the studio. Tidepool takes no commission on bookings.
- Studios still on the legacy Solo plan (€19/month) keep it until 2026-12-31 and then move to
  Starter.
